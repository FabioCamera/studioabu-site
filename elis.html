Elis Face & Beauty

A beauty clinic in a sea of sameness. A visual voice that felt distinctly feminine, yet modern and restrained.

Client
Elis Face & Beauty, Albany, Auckland
Role
Brand Designer
Craft
Adobe Suite
Scope
Identity, logo, print, social, typography
Scroll
I

The brief

Elis came in search of a visual voice. The beauty world is crowded and repetitive, so the challenge was to find space for simplicity.

II

The shape

The answer was a single shape: soft as a leaf, curved like a woman's nail. That subtle form became the heart of the brand, wrapped in gentle gradients and hues that whisper calm, care, elegance and quiet confidence.

Soft as a leaf, curved like a nail.

III

On paper

Brochures for the clinic's face, beauty and services menus, and a business card that carries the leaf front and back.

IV

On social

The social approach blends organic, everyday content showing the clinic's work and Elis' personal vision with quotes that speak to the audience's emotional, mental and spiritual journeys. A space that feels real, inspiring and connected to who they are.
