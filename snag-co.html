03 / 06 • Case study

The Snag Co

A Mount Maunganui sausage boutique with great product and plenty of packaging stock. The brief: look better on the shelf without wasting the stock.

Client
The Snag Co, Mount Maunganui
Role
Graphic Designer
Craft
Adobe Suite
Scope
Packaging, labels, brand system, point of sale
Scroll
I

The brief

The Snag Co asked me to design a new packaging system for their signature sausages. I started with a deep dive into competitor research and market trends, looking for smart, cost-effective answers rather than expensive ones.

II

The constraint

They were already using vacuum-sealed transparent and black plastic; a system they'd invested in, with plenty of stock still on hand.

So instead of starting from scratch, I designed around what they had. A complementary label system that elevates the existing packaging, preserves the investment and still gives the brand real presence on the shelf.

Revamping tradition with practicality.

III

The system

A typography system, hand-drawn support illustrations and a forest-green palette that reads as artisan without feeling precious.

IV

The labels

One label architecture, eight flavours. The front sells; the back carries the nutritional information without breaking the look.

V

At the counter

A roller banner carries the same system to point of sale, so the stall looks as considered as the pack.
