How a project runs

  1. Talk. You tell me about the business, the problem and the deadline. No brief needed yet.
  2. Research. I look at your customers, your competitors and what you already have, so we don't throw away anything that works.
  3. Make. Design, shoot, film, render, build; whatever the idea needs, by the same pair of hands.
  4. Deliver. Finished files, ready for print, web, social or broadcast.
