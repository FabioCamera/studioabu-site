Tauranga Arts Festival

Five months, one festival, zero paid media. An organic social strategy that connected audiences with the artists.

Client
Tauranga Arts Festival 2025
Role
Content Creator
Craft
Adobe Suite • Miro
Duration
5 months
Scroll
▶ Watch the wrap-up film
I

The brief

Across five months I developed and ran the festival's organic social media strategy; producing the video, photography, motion graphics and visual content that connected audiences with the artists and the experience of the festival itself.

It ran alongside the festival's wider campaign presence across the city.

II

The strategy

On Instagram, I dropped hashtags and moved to artist tagging and collaborations. The artists' own audiences became the festival's audience; stronger, more genuine reach.

On Facebook, I went where the people already were. Posts were shared by hand to more than 40 community groups across the Bay of Plenty and beyond, reaching Matamata, Cambridge and Taupō. Reels and links did the heavy lifting.

Pure strategy, authentic content, zero paid media.

III

IV

The content

Countdowns, artist spotlights, daily programme drops and the odd trick to stop the scroll. Made fast, made on brand, made for the phone.

V

The films

Two films bookended the festival: a sizzle reel to sell the breadth of the 2025 programme, and a wrap-up film to keep the atmosphere alive after the last show.
